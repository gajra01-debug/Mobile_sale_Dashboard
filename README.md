## 🚀 Key Takeaways at a Glance

* **Overall Sales:** Generated **$8.06M** across **200 units**, maintaining a healthy **11.68% profit margin** and an average discount of **8.45%**.
* **Customer Feedback:** A strong **4.36 / 5.0** rating across sales channels.

---

## 🔍 What the Data Tells Us

### 1. High Revenue vs. High Volume
* **Revenue Leaders:** **Samsung** ($1.45M) and **Apple** ($1.34M) generated the highest overall revenue, with Google rounding out the top three ($1.00M).
* **Volume Champions:** **Oppo** and **Realme** pushed the highest number of units (27 each), showing strong demand in the mid-range segment.
* **Profitability:** **Vivo** led all brands in profitability with a **17% profit margin**, followed closely by **Apple** at **15%**.

### 2. Region & Channel Trends
* **Top Region:** The **North** region dominates the market, contributing **43.02%** of total sales, with the West following at **21.01%**.
* **Where People Buy:** **Online websites** brought in the most revenue (**28.63%**), closely followed by physical **retail stores** (**25.98%**).

### 3. Payment Preference Insights
* **Cash is still king** for overall profit contribution at **29.86%**.
* Digital payments (Credit Cards, Debit Cards, EMI, Net Banking, and UPI) combined make up the remaining **70%+**, showing steady adoption of electronic payment modes.
